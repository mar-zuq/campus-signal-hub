🚀 CampusSignal
Your college has the information. We make sure you don't miss what matters.

Developed for the DotSlash'26 Rapid Prototyping Challenge.

CampusSignal is an AI-powered triage and logistics layer designed specifically for engineering students balancing intense academics with extracurricular leadership. It solves the critical problem of information overload—where vital KTU lab deadlines, timetable shifts, and fest announcements are scattered across chaotic WhatsApp groups, Instagram posters, and department PDFs.

⚠️ The Problem
Important campus information is unstructured. Students miss deadlines buried in group chats, and student organizers accidentally schedule overlapping events (causing attendance drops) because campus data lives in silos.

💡 The Solution
CampusSignal acts as an intelligent, automated logistics assistant. It parses unstructured noise, flags scheduling conflicts, and automates administrative campus tasks like generating Duty Leave letters.

✨ Key Features
Universal AI Ingest: Paste a messy WhatsApp message or upload a fest poster. The AI instantly extracts the entity, urgency, date, time, and venue, transforming it into a clean, actionable task card.

The Conflict Engine (Coordinator View): Analyzes campus schedules to visually flag overlapping events. If a DotSlash competition clashes with a major Drishti '26 event, the app flashes an amber warning predicting an attendance drop, allowing organizers to pivot.

KTU Utility Hub:

Auto-Duty Leave: Generate a perfectly formatted Duty Leave letter addressed to the CSE HOD in one click, automatically populated with the student's register number, roll number, and event details.

Activity Points Tracker: A visual widget to track progress toward the mandatory 100 KTU activity points.

Smart Triage & Planning:

[✨ Catch Me Up]: Instantly summarizes missed messages into prioritized actions.

[⚡ 2 Hours Free Plan]: Analyzes the schedule to suggest the most urgent deadline to tackle first.

Command Palette: Press Cmd+K to open a Spotlight-style search to instantly find any campus notice, venue, or event.

🎨 Design System
Aesthetic: Hyper-minimalist, productivity-focused dark mode inspired by Linear and Vercel.

Palette: Obsidian background (#0B0F17), elevated dark gray cards (#161B26), and subtle borders (#2E3648).

Accent Badges: Crimson (Critical Actions), Cyan (Schedule Shifts), Emerald (Opportunities), and Amber (Conflict Alerts).
